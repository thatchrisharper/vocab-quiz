    The full charts, for checking your work. hic, haec, hoc = “this / these” (near me); ille, illa, illud = “that / those” (over there). They agree with their noun in case, number, and gender — like an adjective.

    Watch out for these

    • huius, huic, illīus, illī are the same for all three genders in the singular.
    • hīs and illīs cover dative and ablative plural, all genders.
    • Neuter nominative and accusative always match: hoc / hoc, haec / haec, illud / illud, illa / illa.
    • haec is fem. sg. nom. and neut. pl. nom./acc. — illa works the same way.
    • Only the macron separates hoc (nom./acc.) from hōc (abl.), and illa from illā.
